Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have when using our personal expense tracking service (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run the Service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to record.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and request logs, used to keep the Service running reliably.

We do not ask for or store your bank account, card, or UPI credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To store, display, and summarise your expenses for you.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the Service.
  • To contact you about important account or Service updates.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in our application database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share information only when required by law, to protect the rights and safety of Spendly or its users, or with service providers who help us operate the Service under confidentiality obligations.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Spendly uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and to maintain your session. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or sell tracking data. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may stop working.

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Export your expense data.

7. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal and expense data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
